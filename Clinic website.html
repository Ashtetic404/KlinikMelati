About us

Klinik Melati opened in 2014 as a single-doctor practice for the families living around Taman Harmoni. Many of our patients have been with us since the first year, and we now see their children and parents too.

We keep consultations unhurried, explain what we find in plain language, and write down the plan so you can follow it at home. When you need a specialist, we refer you and stay involved in your care.

2 doctors on rotation Since 2014 English, Bahasa Melayu, 中文
